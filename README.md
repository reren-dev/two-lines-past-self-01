Two lines to your past self: a warning about one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered. A judge model compares entries two at a time, and the sharpest cause and effect takes the $2.

The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Don't delete Grandma's voicemail about the soup she left in your fridge - save it.
> Three years from now it will be the only recording of her voice left in the world, and you will play it every time you forget what home sounds like.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
